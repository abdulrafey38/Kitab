Kitab privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Kitab is a reading app that presents the Quran and the Bible side by side. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

What Kitab collects

Kitab has no user accounts and never asks for your name, email address or any other personal details.

The app stores the following on your device only:

  • your reading position, bookmarks, chosen translations, language, theme and text size;
  • which stories and seasonal plans you have completed;
  • audio you have listened to, kept in a cache so it plays again without reloading;
  • whether you have an active Kitab Plus subscription, as reported by the App Store or Google Play.

None of this leaves your phone. If you uninstall the app, it is deleted.

Purchases

Kitab Plus is sold through Apple's App Store and Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment and hold the purchase record under your store account. Kitab receives only a confirmation that a subscription is active; it never sees your payment details.

Network use

  • Audio. Quran recitations stream from Islamic Network (cdn.islamic.network) and Bible readings stream from eBible.org. When you press play, those servers receive the standard information any web request carries, such as your IP address and the file requested. Nothing is sent unless you play audio.
  • Updates. At launch the app asks the App Store or Google Play whether a newer version exists. No personal data is sent.
  • Sharing. When you share a card or text, it is handed to the app you choose, for example WhatsApp. Kitab does not see where it goes.

Everything else in Kitab, including reading, searching and the parallel passages, works fully offline.

Crash reports

If a future version includes crash reporting, it will send only technical details about the crash: device model, operating system version, app version and the error. Never your reading history, bookmarks or anything you typed. This policy will be updated before it is switched on.

Children

Kitab does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
