What Kitab does

  • Reference. Open a story, a law or a theme and see how each book tells it: Noah, Moses, Mary, charity, forgiveness, the Day of Judgment. More than 80 topics, paired passage by passage.
  • Read both books in full. The Quran in Arabic with English, Urdu, Indonesian and Turkish translations. The Bible in English, Arabic, Urdu, Indonesian and Turkish.
  • Listen. Ten reciters for the Quran and an audio Bible, with the text following along.
  • Stories in both books. Twelve guided stories from Adam to Jesus, with no faces or figures, out of respect for both traditions.
  • Send a blessing. Verse cards for a new baby, a wedding, an exam, an illness, a loss, Friday, Sunday, Ramadan, Eid, Lent, Easter and Christmas. One tap to WhatsApp.

Made for both communities

Kitab never claims one book came from the other. It says “the Quran says” and “the Bible says”, and lets you see for yourself how much they share.
